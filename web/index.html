        How picks are made and closed

        Only you can see or change this list: it's stored owner-only and never published to the public site. Entry is the close of the latest closed daily candle on the day you approve. Stop = entry − 2 × ATR(14, daily), locked at approval. R = (price − entry) ÷ (entry − stop). Stop hit: a daily low since approval went below the stop. Review: a proven bearish signal (consistent edge, 100+ cases) fired on the pair since approval.

              Signal scorecard

              What happened after each signal since 2017, entering at the signal candle's close. It counts only signals whose indicators were warmed up, on pairs with at least $100k volume that day, and includes coins that were later delisted. Edge is the signal's average 30-day return minus the baseline (every liquid pair-day). For bearish signals, a negative edge means the signal correctly warned of weakness. Consistent means the edge has the same sign in 2017–2021 and 2022 onward, with at least 30 cases in each period.

              Setup Lab

              Test a trading rule on 2 years of daily candles, in your browser

              Describe an entry with up to four conditions (all must be true at a daily close), choose how the trade is managed, and run it on the most liquid coins. Entries fill at the next day's open; stops and targets are checked against each day's high and low (if both are touched the same day, the stop counts first); costs are 0.1% fee plus 0.1% slippage per side. Only coins listed today are included and the window is 2 years, so results are optimistic: anything promising deserves the full-history test (since 2017, delisted coins included) before real money.
